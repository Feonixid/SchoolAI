# EduAI Video Script (3 Minutes)

## Gemma 4 Good Hackathon Submission

---

### SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM (0:00 - 0:30)

**Visual**: Split screen - left shows modern classroom with iPads, right shows rural classroom with old laptops

**Narrator**: 
"Two classrooms. Same planet. Different worlds. 

2.6 billion people lack reliable internet. While some students have AI tutors at their fingertips, others are left behind.

The digital divide in education isn't closing. It's widening."

**Text on screen**: "2.6 billion people lack internet access"

---

### SCENE 2: THE SOLUTION REVEAL (0:30 - 1:00)

**Visual**: Close-up of an old laptop (think N4500 processor, 4GB RAM). Screen shows EduAI loading. No WiFi icon.

**Narrator**:
"Meet EduAI. An AI tutor that works anywhere, on anything, without internet.

Powered by Gemma 4 running locally via Ollama. No cloud. No API calls. No data leaving your device."

**Visual**: App opens, shows clean chat interface

**Text on screen**: "100% Offline. 100% Private."

---

### SCENE 3: HARDWARE ADAPTATION (1:00 - 1:30)

**Visual**: Settings panel showing detected hardware: "CPU: 2 cores (Low tier)" "RAM: 4GB"

**Narrator**:
"EduAI automatically detects your hardware and optimizes itself.

On this old laptop with just 4GB of RAM, it uses Gemma 3 2B with compressed KV cache.

On a high-end desktop, it scales up to Gemma 3 12B with 32K context.

Same app. Same experience. Scaled to your reality."

**Visual**: Show the profile dropdown changing between Ultra and Minimal

**Text on screen**: "Adapts to ANY hardware"

---

### SCENE 4: MULTI-LANGUAGE DEMO (1:30 - 2:00)

**Visual**: User changes language to Spanish. Asks a math question. AI responds in Spanish.

**Narrator**:
"10 languages supported. The interface in one language, AI responses in another.

Perfect for multilingual classrooms where students learn in their native language."

**Visual**: Show language settings, then a question in English with response in Spanish

**Text on screen**: "10 Languages. One Platform."

---

### SCENE 5: MULTIMODAL & ACCESSIBILITY (2:00 - 2:30)

**Visual**: User uploads a photo of a math equation. AI analyzes and explains step-by-step.

**Narrator**:
"Upload a photo of homework. Gemma 4's vision capabilities analyze and explain.

Built-in accessibility: high contrast, dyslexia font, text-to-speech.

Because education should be for everyone."

**Visual**: Quick montage of accessibility features - high contrast toggle, font size slider, TTS button

**Text on screen**: "Accessible by Design"

---

### SCENE 6: IMPACT & VISION (2:30 - 3:00)

**Visual**: Montage of different use cases - student studying, teacher in classroom, person with visual impairment using screen reader

**Narrator**:
"EduAI proves AI education doesn't need the cloud.

Privacy by design. Works offline. Adapts to any hardware. Speaks your language.

The technology exists. The models are open. The impact is waiting to happen.

This is EduAI. Education AI for everyone, everywhere."

**Visual**: Logo animation with tagline

**Text on screen**: "EduAI - Education AI for Everyone"
